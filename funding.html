What is 942-STOP?

The Rhode Island Hope & Recovery Line, 401-942-7867 (942-STOP), screens people for recovery-housing financial support and placement in state-funded certified homes. It is a screening and referral service, not the name of a grant awarded to HOS.

The state describes an 18-month recovery-housing grant program for eligible individuals. Program eligibility, residence approval, available funding and placement are determined through the applicable state process. Some criteria, including Rhode Island residency requirements, can differ from HOS inquiry eligibility.
