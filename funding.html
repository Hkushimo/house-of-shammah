You do not need a grant to apply.

We welcome applicants with income from employment, SSI or SSDI, and people seeking 942-STOP housing assistance, including those on a grant waiting list. You do not need a grant to apply.

HOS-RI is preparing to pursue RICARES certification and approval to accept 942-STOP-funded placements. That funding is not yet available through HOS-RI. Admission depends on eligibility, bed availability and payment arrangements. If donor assistance becomes available, we will explain eligibility and how to request it.

Waiting for 942-STOP assistance?

You can still contact HOS-RI to discuss your situation and self-pay options. To be screened for state-funded recovery housing, call 401-942-7867 (942-STOP). Contacting HOS-RI does not replace the separate 942-STOP screening and placement process.

Funding depends on program eligibility, required approvals and availability. Applying to HOS-RI does not guarantee admission or funding.
