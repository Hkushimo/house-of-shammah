Nine returns.
One more beginning.

For Elena, the tenth attempt does not erase the first nine. It becomes another opportunity to ask for help, build a routine and let someone walk beside her.

Read Elena’s story

The short story

She came back to herself.

Nine times, Elena had tried to begin again. Nine times, life had become more than she knew how to carry. Each return left her wondering whether anyone would still believe in her.

On the morning of her tenth attempt, she stared at a number on her phone. This time, she pressed call.

“I need somewhere to begin,” she said.

When Elena reached House of Shammah, nobody asked her to perform confidence. A woman smiled, offered water and helped her find her space.

“You don’t have to explain everything today. Let’s get you settled.”

There were no instant transformations. There were ordinary mornings: making her bed, asking for support, keeping an appointment, joining a conversation. When a hard day came, Elena practiced telling someone before she disappeared into it.

One evening, a new woman stood at the door with a suitcase. Elena recognized the hesitation.

She reached for a bottle of water.

“Welcome,” she said. “Let’s get you settled.”

For the first time in a long while, Elena could picture tomorrow. It did not need to be perfect to hold something bright.

Hope can begin again.
