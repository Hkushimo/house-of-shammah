Connection without alcohol

A little refreshment. A lot of connection.

An alcohol-free gathering can feel generous, colorful and relaxed. A warm welcome begins with making room for one another.

Something for everyone

Make the welcome personal.

Offer chilled water first, then ask what the guest enjoys. Tea, coffee, sparkling water and fruit-infused water can make a simple visit feel thoughtful.

Respect individual preferences, caffeine choices and ingredients. Alcohol-free options belong alongside conversation, games and kindness.

Simple gathering ideas

Bright flavors. Easy company.

Citrus & mint water

Chilled water with orange slices and fresh mint. Keep plain water available too.

Berry sparkling refresher

Sparkling water with a few berries and a squeeze of lime. Serve without alcohol or spirit substitutes.

A cozy tea table

A choice of herbal tea, decaf or coffee, with ingredients and sweeteners offered separately.

The best ingredient is attention.

Invite someone to join a card game, ask about a favorite song or share a quiet conversation. Participation is a choice; a comfortable place to sit can be a welcome in itself.
