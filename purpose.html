Read our statements

Our vision

To help individuals who show the desire to transform their lives, starting with providing a great, peaceful, encouraging home to activate and enable their stability, personal growth, increased independence, and lasting community—helping every individual move forward with purpose, confidence, and hope.

Our mission

To provide a safe, supportive, and empowering environment where individuals can stabilize, rebuild, and develop the confidence and skills needed to create a more independent, productive, and purposeful life starting with a place to call home.

Our values

Dignity • Respect • Integrity • Accountability • Compassion • Fairness • Growth • Community. We believe every person deserves to be heard, treated justly, respected in their dignity, and given the opportunity to grow.

Our ethics

We honor each resident’s dignity, privacy, choices and recovery journey. We act honestly, explain financial commitments clearly, apply expectations fairly, and maintain respectful boundaries. We reject exploitation, discrimination, harassment and retaliation. We listen to concerns, take responsibility for our actions, and address harm. We offer a safe, supportive home and recovery support within our nonmedical role.

A different kind of welcome

Our Foundation

Our Foundation is the idea behind how we welcome a new arrival: begin with warmth, dignity and a practical act of kindness.

A friendly introduction. A bottle of water. A welcome basket. Help finding the resident’s own space and settling in. These thoughtful moments express the welcome we are building at HOS RI.

This welcoming idea sits beneath our four main statements—vision, mission, values and ethics.
