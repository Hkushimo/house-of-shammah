Dignity · understanding · connection

Mental health belongs in the conversation.

You deserve to be heard with respect. Asking for support is welcome here.

Mental health conditions and substance use disorders can occur together. Each person’s experience is different. A qualified healthcare or mental-health professional can help the resident explore appropriate care.

Our role at House of Shammah RI

HOS RI provides nonmedical housing and recovery support. It does not provide medical advice or care, diagnose conditions, prescribe medication or replace treatment. With the resident’s permission and any required written authorization, HOS RI can coordinate with the resident’s healthcare and mental-health teams.

Take one practical step

  1. Tell a trusted support person what would help you feel heard and supported.
  2. Contact your licensed healthcare or mental-health provider for questions about symptoms, treatment or medication.
  3. Ask HOS RI about residential arrangements, community routines and coordination with existing support.

Please keep medical histories and private details out of Facebook comments and public messages.
