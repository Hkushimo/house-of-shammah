Privacy & image information

Respect begins with clarity.

How this website handles the applicant profile, appointment requests and illustrative images.

Short contact request

The Start here form prepares a message in your email app containing only the name, callback phone, optional email address and residential setting you enter. Review and send it there. This website does not send the request automatically or store the details on a server. Do not include health, financial or other sensitive details in ordinary email. You can call HOS RI instead.

Applicant profile

The profile is completed in your browser. This website does not send it to HOS RI, save it to a server or store it in browser storage. Closing or reloading the page can clear it. Printing or saving a copy places that copy under your control; use a private device and protect the resulting file.

Appointment requests

The appointment form prepares an email in your own email application containing the name, callback number, date, time and conversation type you enter. It does not send automatically. Your email provider handles delivery when you choose to send. Please do not include health, medication, insurance account or financial details in ordinary email.

Actual photographs and illustrations

The community and design images on this website were created with AI using property references and design concepts. The people are fictional and some furnishings and improvements are proposed. They do not establish current furnishings, completed renovations, lawful bed capacity, actual residents or recovery outcomes. Casual and photoshoot comparisons show styling concepts, not clinical before-and-after results.

Training and residence certification

Carol Ferrer reports completing NARR training. An owner’s individual training certificate is different from certification of the residence. House of Shammah RI has not yet received residence certification or grant approval.

Housing and recovery support

HOS RI provides nonmedical housing and recovery support. It does not provide medical advice or care. Coordination with healthcare and mental-health teams requires the resident’s permission and any required written authorization.

Application invitation

Your browser keeps a session-only list of reading pages and whether you have seen or dismissed the follow-up invitation. These preferences contain no applicant answers and are not sent to HOS RI. The invitation can be closed and does not block reading.

Questions

Call 401-442-3638 or email support@houseofshammah.com for general questions. Ask how to provide sensitive information privately.
